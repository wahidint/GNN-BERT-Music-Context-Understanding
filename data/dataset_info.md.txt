# Dataset Information

## MusicCaps Dataset

The MusicCaps dataset was used for the BERT-based music context understanding task.

It contains:
- Audio clips
- Human-written music captions
- Semantic descriptions of musical characteristics

The dataset was used for:
- BERT-based multi-label classification
- Textual music context understanding


## Free Music Archive (FMA) Dataset

The FMA dataset was used for graph-based music representation learning.

It contains:
- Music tracks
- Genre labels
- Audio features

The dataset was used for:
- GNN-based structural learning
- Music genre classification


## Dataset Availability

The original datasets are not included in this repository because of their large size.

The datasets should be downloaded separately before running the notebook.
